About

I'm a Computer Science (AI) student at IGDTUW, focused on the intersection of machine learning and digital forensics — where a model's output has to hold up as evidence, not just a prediction.

My work spans hybrid anomaly detection for banking fraud, Android forensic analysis for SIM-swap and GPS-spoofing detection, and phishing classification using fine-tuned language models. I care about systems that are explainable and legally admissible, not just accurate on paper.

Outside project work, I mentor students in applied ML through the Google Developers Club, and completed a national forensic-readiness bootcamp under India's ISEA (MeitY) program.
